# Product Brief

## Product Name

IWBF Classifier App

## Purpose

The app helps a wheelchair basketball classifier document player observations during classification training and competition games.

The current paper workflow uses one observation worksheet per team. The app should preserve the speed of paper while adding:

- organized competition records;
- import from entry-list documents;
- handwritten S Pen notes;
- editable player data;
- optional video evidence;
- exportable local archives.

## Core Problem

During a competition, the classifier may observe up to 24 players across two teams, with only 10 on court at a time. The classifier must switch quickly between player records, write observations by hand, and sometimes preserve video evidence for later panel discussion.

Paper is fast but disposable. A generic note app is flexible but not organized by competition, team, player, Sport Class, and decision status.

## Guiding Principles

1. The app must feel like a smart classification clipboard, not an admin system.
2. Handwriting is the primary observation input.
3. Player switching must be faster than changing paper sheets.
4. Imported rosters are suggestions, not locked truth.
5. Everything must be editable during the competition.
6. Video is optional and must never block note-taking.
7. Local storage and export are enough for the first version.

## Important Fields

The most important imported or manually entered fields are:

- Team
- Player name
- Uniform number
- Imported Sport Class
- Sport Class Status (SCS)

Optional fields:

- IWBF ID
- Date of birth
- MIC notes
- Health condition
- Impairment
- Panel notes

Classification workflow fields:

- Starting Sport Class
- My Opinion
- Final Sport Class
- Observation Status
- Freehand observation notes
- Video evidence markers
- Local video attachments

## Observation Status

Use a simple workflow status per player:

- Not Observed
- Quick Check
- Observe
- Discuss
- Finalized

These are app workflow labels, not official IWBF statuses.

## Success Criteria

The app succeeds if the classifier can:

- import a competition roster in minutes;
- start a game with incomplete data;
- correct any roster mistake at any time;
- switch players in one tap;
- write naturally with the S Pen;
- mark a video moment without losing the live game;
- export a clean competition archive.
